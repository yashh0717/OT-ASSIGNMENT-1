**Big-M Simplex (`big_m_simplex.py`)** — Furniture Workshop Profit Maximization:

A furniture workshop manufactures two products: Tables and Chairs. Each table requires 2 hours of cutting and 1 hour of finishing, while each chair requires 1 hour of cutting and 3 hours of finishing. The workshop has 100 cutting-hours and 120 finishing-hours available, and must use at least 45 units of raw material. Market demand requires a minimum of 15 tables and 10 chairs to be produced. With profit of $70 per table and $50 per chair, the objective is to decide how many tables and chairs to produce so that profit is maximized while respecting all cutting, finishing, material, and demand constraints. The optimum production plan is 36 tables and 28 chairs, yielding a maximum profit of $3,920.

**VAM + MODI (`transportation_vam_modi.py`)** — Minimum-Cost Distribution Problem:

Three factories, S1, S2 and S3, with supplies of 15, 25 and 10 units respectively, must ship goods to four destinations, D1, D2, D3 and D4, whose demands are 5, 15, 15 and 15 units. Shipping one unit from each source to each destination incurs a known cost per unit, and every destination's demand must be exactly met out of the available supply. The objective is to allocate shipments between factories and destinations so that total transportation cost is minimized. Vogel's Approximation Method first constructs a good starting allocation, which the MODI (Modified Distribution) method then improves iteratively until the minimum total shipping cost of $435 is reached.
